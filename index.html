Biography

The Photographer
Born in 1980 in Nairobi Kenya, Fiona Mbugua grew interest in art and photography at an early age. Through numerous safaris and travels, she was able to develop her love for art and as a teenager she was able to catch the eye of a local magazine. Her work then got traction quickly and she was able to grow to the ranks of a professional photographer, acquiring numerous awards. She has shown how photography can go beyond simply taking pictures and she remains one of the most respected and influential photographers in the world, inspiring many aspiring photographers to pursue their passion.

About the Gallery

A curated collection of photographic moments that tell stories beyond words. This gallery represents a journey through diverse landscapes and cultures and captures the precious moment in time. This website serves as a digital gallery to someone who appreciates the visual medium and beauty in the mundane.
